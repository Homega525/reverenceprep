**Reverence Senior High School** has officially **opened admissions for the 2026/2027** academic year, welcoming prospective students seeking admission into SHS 1, SHS 2 and SHS 3.

The school is accepting applications across all available courses and is inviting parents, guardians and students to begin the admission process.

Reverence SHS also serves as a WASSCE and NOV/DEC examination centre, providing students and private candidates with access to a convenient examination location.

For students who require accommodation, hostel facilities are available.

The school is ***located at Offinso-Ash***.

Prospective students and parents who would like further information about admission requirements, available programmes, fees or hostel arrangements can contact the school on:

**0595536374 / 0247873196**

Reverence Senior High School encourages interested applicants to make enquiries early and complete the necessary admission arrangements ahead of the 2026/2027 academic year.
