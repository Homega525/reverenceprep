The school gates opened this morning to welcome both returning and new students, along with parents, guardians, and staff, for what promises to be a productive and rewarding term.

Management extends a warm welcome to all learners and their families, and encourages every student to settle in quickly and approach the new academic year with focus, discipline, and enthusiasm.

Parents and guardians are kindly reminded to ensure that their wards report to school on time and come fully prepared with all the necessary learning materials for the term.

Staff members have also been urged to continue giving off their best in delivering quality education and upholding the values that Reverence Schools stands for.

We wish all students, teachers, and parents a successful and fulfilling academic year ahead.
